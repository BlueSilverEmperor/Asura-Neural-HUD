Ingredients:
    - 2 cups (473 ml) of all-purpose flour
    - 1 tsp (5 ml) baking powder
    - 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) salt
    - 1 cup (237 ml) butter, softened
    - 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
    - 4 large eggs
    - 1 tsp (5 ml) vanilla extract
    - 3 tbsp (42 ml) milk

- Instructions:
    1. Preheat oven to 180°C/350°F.
    2. Grease and flour a cake tin.
    3. In a bowl, mix together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
    4. In another bowl, cream together butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
    5. Beat in eggs one at a time.
    6. Stir in vanilla extract.
    7. Gradually add flour mixture to egg mixture alternately with milk, beginning and ending with flour.
    8. Pour batter into prepared tin.
    9. Bake for 20-30 minutes or until cake tester inserted comes out clean.
    10. Cool in pan for 10 minutes before removing from pan to cool completely.
